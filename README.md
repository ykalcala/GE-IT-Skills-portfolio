# 4. Critical Reflection on AI Limitations

While AI systems can rapidly synthesize large volumes of literature, they may generate unsupported numerical claims, misattribute findings, or combine projected targets with actual outcomes. Several statements initially appeared credible because they aligned with broader development narratives, yet lacked verifiable empirical support when checked against primary documents.

The audit process demonstrated that AI-generated summaries should be treated as preliminary research aids rather than authoritative evidence. Human verification remains essential for confirming statistics, validating citations, identifying contextual nuances, and preventing the inclusion of fabricated or misleading information in policy-oriented outputs.

---

# 5. Research Integrity Checklist

Before publication, verify that:

* All statistics are traceable to primary sources.
* Every citation exists and is accessible.
* No AI-generated references are fabricated.
* Conclusions accurately reflect source findings.
* Contradictory evidence is acknowledged.
* Policy recommendations are supported by verified literature.
* Human review is documented.

---

# Key Lesson

AI accelerates literature synthesis, but human auditing safeguards academic credibility. The combination of automated summarization and structured verification creates a research workflow that is both efficient and empirically defensible.

## 5. Human Analytical Narrative
The "Why" Factor
The visualizations reveal a consistent increase in municipal waste generation from 2020 to 2025 across the Davao Region. While automated analysis identifies growth trends, human interpretation provides important context.
Population growth, expanding commercial activity, and increasing household consumption likely contributed to the rising waste volumes observed during the reporting period. The divergence in waste diversion performance among municipalities suggests differences in local policy implementation, infrastructure investment, and community participation.
From a regional planning perspective, the data highlights the importance of strengthening materials recovery facilities (MRFs), expanding segregation programs, and increasing environmental education initiatives. Municipalities demonstrating higher diversion rates may provide practical models for neighboring LGUs seeking to improve compliance with solid waste management objectives.
These findings support evidence-based budget allocation decisions focused on waste reduction infrastructure, landfill diversion strategies, and long-term environmental sustainability throughout Mindanao.
________________________________________
Key Policy Insight
Data cleaning improves reliability, visualization improves comprehension, and human interpretation provides contextual meaning. Effective regional planning requires all three components to transform raw datasets into actionable public policy intelligence.
